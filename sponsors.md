# Call for Sponsors

We are seeking the sponsorship of companies for the
[39th International Conference on Scalable Scientific Data Management (SSDBM 2027)](https://ssdbm.org/2027/)
to be held at NTU@one-north, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, on July 19 – 21, 2027.

We offer the following sponsorship packages:

### Bronze Sponsor (SGD 1,000)

- Logo displayed on the conference website and the conference program

### Silver Sponsor (SGD 2,000)

- Logo displayed on the conference website and the conference program
- Free registration for 1 participant

### Gold Sponsor (SGD 4,000)

- Logo displayed on the conference website and the conference program
- Flyers/giveaways in handout bags
- Free registration for 2 participants

### Platinum Sponsor (SGD 6,000+)

- Logo displayed on the conference website and the conference program
- Flyers/giveaways in handout bags
- Free registration for 3 participants
- An invited talk slot at the conference

Please contact [Chen Wang](mailto:chen.wang@ntu.edu.sg) and [Youyou Lu](mailto:luyouyou@tsinghua.edu.cn) about sponsoring.
